

# Business Insights – Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## 1. Business Objective

Identify customer segments with higher churn rates, understand the observed patterns, and recommend areas for improving customer retention.

## 2. Overall Churn

- **Total customers:** 7,043
- **Churned:** 1,869 (26.54%)
- **Retained:** 5,174 (73.46%)
- **Average monthly charges:** 64.76

## 3. Key Business Findings

### Contract Type
- **Finding:** Month-to-month customers had 42.71% churn, compared with 11.27% for one-year and 2.83% for two-year contracts.
- **Business Insight:** Review longer-term plan options and suitable retention incentives for month-to-month customers.

### Customer Tenure
- **Finding:** Churned customers had an average tenure of 17.98 months, compared with 37.57 months for retained customers.
- **Business Insight:** Strengthen onboarding and engagement during customers' early months.

### Payment Method
- **Finding:** Electronic-check users had 45.29% churn, while automatic bank transfer and credit card users had 16.71% and 15.24%, respectively.
- **Business Insight:** Investigate the payment experience of electronic-check users and make automatic payment options easier to access.

### Internet Service
- **Finding:** Fiber-optic customers had 41.89% churn, compared with 18.96% for DSL and 7.40% for customers without internet service.
- **Business Insight:** Investigate pricing, service quality, and customer feedback among fiber-optic customers.

### Paperless Billing
- **Finding:** Customers using paperless billing had 33.57% churn, compared with 16.33% among customers not using it.
- **Business Insight:** Review the billing experience and customer preferences among paperless billing users.

### Support Services
- **Finding:** Churn was 41.64% among customers without TechSupport versus 15.17% with it. For OnlineSecurity, the rates were 41.77% without the service and 14.61% with it.
- **Business Insight:** Review customer support needs and whether available support and security services meet customer expectations.

### Monthly Charges
- **Finding:** Churned customers had average monthly charges of 74.44, compared with 61.27 for retained customers.
- **Business Insight:** Review higher-charge customers' plans and whether the services they receive provide sufficient value.

### Customer Profile
- **Finding:** Senior customers had 41.68% churn, compared with 23.61% for non-senior customers. Customers without dependents had 31.28% churn, compared with 15.45% for those with dependents.
- **Business Insight:** Examine the needs and feedback of these customer groups before planning targeted retention activities.

## 4. Business Recommendations

1. Improve onboarding and engagement for new customers.
2. Review contract options for month-to-month customers.
3. Investigate pricing and service concerns among fiber-optic customers.
4. Review the payment and billing experience.
5. Evaluate support-service needs among customers without TechSupport and OnlineSecurity.

## 5. Conclusion

Contract type, tenure, payment method, internet service, billing, monthly charges, and customer profile showed notable differences in churn. These findings help identify priority customer segments for further investigation and targeted retention efforts.

**Note:** The analysis identifies associations, not proven causes. The effectiveness of the recommendations has not been measured.
